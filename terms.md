## Safety, which is the part that matters

The machine described on this site holds a mains powered drill over a pot of fat at 250 degrees Fahrenheit. It was built by one person for one kitchen. These pages are a record of what was done, not a manual, and no part of it has been tested or certified for anyone else to copy.

Anyone building something similar does so entirely at their own risk. Hot butter oil causes serious burns, and mains wiring done badly can kill. Get the electrical side checked by someone qualified.
