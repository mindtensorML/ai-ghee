AI Ghee Making

This is the story of how we made traditional ghee with a homemade rig using AI.

The rig churns cream until it breaks into butter and buttermilk. I did not watch for that moment with my eyes. I let the rig notice it. When the cream splits, the work suddenly gets easier. That change is the signal. The rig stops at the right time, then I cook the butter down into ghee.

The food is only half the point. The other half is this: a dumb machine can be given a little brain. Not a voice. Not a personality. Just a way to feel when something in front of it has changed, and to act on that.

Most tools only move when we tell them to. This one pays attention. A simple rig. A simple signal. And all at once the machine is not only spinning. It is deciding.

That is the idea I wanted at the center of this video. Ghee is what came out of the jar. The real experiment was giving a plain machine a way to know.

Next I want the machine to try again. Change a step. Taste the result. Keep what works. Do that over and over until it finds a better ghee than I would have made on my own.
